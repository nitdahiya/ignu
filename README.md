# 🎓 IGNOU Super-Bot

A full-featured Telegram bot for **IGNOU students** — grade cards, study material,
solved assignments, expected papers, PYQs, datesheets, official notices and more,
all inside one chat.

🤖 **Try it live:** [@hignubot](https://t.me/hignubot) · 📢 **Channel:** [@hignou](https://t.me/hignou)

> ⚠️ Unofficial project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by IGNOU.

## ✨ What the bot does

- 📊 **Grade Card / Results** — check your grade card with enrolment number + program code, plus assignment status and term-end backup results
- 🔔 **Result Tracking** — get pinged automatically when new marks are posted
- 👤 **Smart Profiles** — save your details once; the bot remembers you and shows quick subject shortcuts
- 📚 **Study Material** — search and download course books and blocks, single or bulk
- 📝 **Solved Assignments** — complete solved assignments in English and Hindi, sized to the question marks
- 📄 **Expected Papers** — predicted question papers prepared from recent trends
- 📜 **Previous Year Papers** — question papers from the latest sessions, single or bulk download
- 📅 **Exam Datesheet** — your personal exam schedule from just your program code
- 📢 **Latest Notices** — IGNOU announcements, deadlines and updates without leaving Telegram
- 🎁 **Refer & Earn** — 1 free premium use, unlimited access after inviting a friend

## 🤖 Commands

- `/start` — open the main menu
- `/help` — detailed help
- `/notices` — latest IGNOU announcements
- `/setup` — profile and tracking status
